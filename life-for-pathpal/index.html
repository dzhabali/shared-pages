life

For Jennie and the PathPal team

Location became one layer of my whole chronicle

Thank you for writing and asking real questions. I left after the export bug, and by then my goal had grown. I don't want an app that only shows where I was. I want one feed of my life: where I went, how I moved, what I did, who I talked to, what I ate, how I slept, what I read. So I built that for myself and called it Life. Here is how it works, with screenshots. I hope some of it is useful for PathPal.

Max Dzhabali · Vienna · October 2026

Sleep 7 h Work 9 h 45 Practice 30 min Learning 30 min People 1 h 15 Rest 15 min Video 2 h 30 Chores & road 45 min Not tracked 1 h 30 · 7 October 2026, built automatically

Your two questions

What do you enjoy seeing most when you look back?

The day itself, as a story

Not routes or statistics on their own. I open a date and want to see the whole day: where I was, how I got there, the photos I took, the meetings in my calendar, what I wrote that evening. Movement stats are useful as totals for a week, month or year. And I love going back in time: "what was I doing on this date 1, 5, 10 years ago".

Why OwnTracks specifically?

The points go straight to my own server

  • Every location point lands on my private server in real time. There is no export step that can break.
  • Open format. My scripts read the raw points and merge them with the rest of my data.
  • It sends the iPhone's own motion sensor (walking, car) and pedometer steps along with the points.
  • It is free and keeps working. I don't depend on support reply times.

OwnTracks has no interface worth mentioning. That's fine: all the viewing happens in Life.

This is the reason I don't need a separate location app. The place and the route are right there, next to everything else that happened.

03 · Around movement

Health, time, and going back in time

Health

Built like Apple Health

Cards by group: activity, body, gait, hearing, mindfulness. Tap one for a chart over a week, month, 6 months, a year or all time.

Data comes from an Apple Health export plus a daily iOS Shortcut that sends fresh values to my server. Weight comes from a Bluetooth smart scale. For days before OwnTracks, steps come from Health.

Time · same day

Where the 24 hours went

Every 15 minutes gets one category, chosen automatically from all sources. Road time comes from the movement data, sleep from my meditation tracker, work from the Mac and the calendar. On 7 October 22.5 of 24 hours were classified without me typing anything.

Time machine · 7 October, every year

This day, all the years

One date across every year I have data for: the city, photos of that day, and what I wrote. Vienna in 2026, Belek in Turkey in 2024, Vienna again in 2022. One tap opens the full day.

This is what I meant by "going back in time" in my email.

What it doesn't capture

  • No heart rate and no sleep stages: I don't wear an Apple Watch.
  • For old PathPal history there is no motion sensor data, so the mode is guessed from speed and marked "≈".
  • When the Mac is asleep, the page shows the last data it sent. Most sources are physically on the Mac.
  • Some of every day stays "not tracked", usually 1–2 hours.

07 · For your roadmap

Ideas from one long-time user

You asked what PathPal could do better. These are the things I ended up building for myself. Take whatever is useful.

Steps on the main screen

Next to distance, not below the fold. Daily average and total for week, month and year.

Built in Life: top row of every Movement view

Choose your modes once

Let me remove modes I never use, like cycling. Then every misdetection is fixed for good, not one trip at a time.

Built in Life: no bicycle, faster than walking = car

On this day

The same date across all years: city, distance, route. PathPal already has years of data per user. This makes it come alive.

Built in Life: Time machine

Photos on the timeline

Show the photos from the camera roll at the place and time they were taken. A stop with a photo is remembered. A stop without one is just a pin.

Built in Life: photos inside each place

Continuous export

A setting that sends every point to the user's own URL or an iCloud folder as it happens. A big one-time export is fragile. The export bug is what made me leave.

Why I chose OwnTracks

Import from other apps

Let new users bring their history from Moves, MoveX, Google Timeline or Arc. I merged two other apps' history into one line myself.

Built in Life: MoveX + PathPal history

Calendar and Health in the day

Read-only, on the timeline: "11:23 car to the café, 15:30 the event in my calendar". Location makes sense next to what it was for.

Built in Life: the day feed

My names for places

Let me name a place once ("Mom's", "the studio") and use that name everywhere. Detect Home from where I spend the nights, also on trips.

Built in Life: own names → Home → map data

Show coverage

Show which days have data and which don't, and how confident the mode is. I trust a tracker more when it is open about its gaps.

Built in Life: Archive ribbon, "≈" marks
